    It runs ONLY at container start / redeploy. It does not watch or restart anything.

    resident owner of "port 9222, profile /tmp/bu-cdp":

    The package exists (/opt/hermes/venv/.../browser_use/...) and is what starts Chromium on

    the pre_push_gate harness whitelist currently anchors on the raw "--remote-debugging-port"
    argv match, not on a live parent — i.e. the chromium tree is classified as resident service
    with nobody actually owning its lifecycle.

    Chromium alive"): if /json/version is unreachable it execs CHROME with --user-data-dir

        unless the system /usr/bin/chromium is used.
    Net: the intended safety net is installed but dormant and mis-pathed.
