# Buzan, «Head First» — the book behind «Суперинтеллект»

Identified 2026-09-11 (research: `~/.hermes/dvizhok/research/buzan-superintellect.md`; full source list there).

## Identification

Russian «Суперинтеллект» = **Tony Buzan, *Head First: 10 Ways to Tap into Your Natural Genius***
(Thorsons, 2000; hardcover 2002 ISBN **9780007132850**; paperback *Head First!: You're Smarter
Than You Think* **9780722540466**; US ed. 9780007160013).
Russian editions by «Попурри» (Minsk): 985-483-157-4 (2005), 985-483-626-6 (2005),
978-985-483-975-2 (2007), 978-985-15-0326-7 (2008), 978-985-15-2095-0 (2014).
Full Russian title: «Суперинтеллект. 10 способов открыть свою врождённую гениальность» — a literal
translation of the English subtitle, which is what confirms the match.

## Structure

Ten chapters = ten intelligences, in three groups:

- **Creative & Emotional:** Creative (*Create Yourself*) · Personal (*You and You*) ·
  Social (*You and Them*) · Spiritual (*Heaven Knows!*)
- **Bodily:** Physical (*Body Talk*) · Sensual · Sexual
- **Traditional IQ:** Numerical · Spatial · Verbal

Each chapter: theory + cases + **Brain Workout (ten exercises)** + a self-test.

## Core methods

- **Multiple intelligences instead of one IQ** — a profile of ten, not a coefficient.
- **Develop from the weakest link first** — synergy: the weak faculty pulls the others up.
- **Mind maps / radiant thinking** — central image, branches, key words, colour, association.
- **Associative-imagery encoding** of links while building the map.
- **Mnemonics / memory techniques** for retention.
- **Creative practice** (*Create Yourself*): generate and then actually execute ideas.
- **Bodily practice:** physical condition, sharpening the five senses, sexual intelligence.
- **IQ skills:** numbers, space, words/vocabulary.

## Metrics it proposes

A self-test per faculty at the end of each chapter + the ten-exercise Brain Workout. Explicitly
rejects the single IQ figure in favour of a ten-scale profile.

## On self-observation

The *Personal Intelligence* chapter (*You and You*) covers self-awareness, emotions and goals;
*Spiritual* (*Heaven Knows!*) covers meaning and one's place in the world. Mind maps are offered
as a daily thinking tool. There is **no dedicated diary/journal practice** in the book — the
journaling half of our protocol is ours, not Buzan's.

## Criticism (must be carried alongside the map)

- The «we use only 1–10 % of the brain» trope is a **myth**.
- Buzan is accused of pseudoscience and hyperbole (Joshua Foer's critique; Donald Clark's blog).
- The «expert» authority is itself contested.

⇒ Use the STRUCTURE (ten scales, weakest-link, workouts) and skip the mythology. Never repeat
«10 % of the brain» or similar claims as fact.
